Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

In short: Align works on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, receive, share or sell your personal information. What you write and create stays on your phone.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Align mobile app for Android ("Align", "the app") handles information. Align is published by Align apps ("we", "us", "our"), based in India. By using Align you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not run a server for Align, and the app does not create accounts or ask you to sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics or crash reports. The app does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it does not use cookies to follow you.

2. Information stored only on your device

To work, Align saves the following on your phone. This information never leaves your device through us, and we cannot see it:

  • your first name or nickname, goals and settings;
  • journal entries and drafts, affirmations, scripts and program progress;
  • vision boards, including photos you choose to add;
  • subliminal audio you create, including any audio files you import;
  • your reminder preferences and your premium status.

Align's app data is excluded from Android cloud backup (the app is set not to allow backup). Your data therefore exists only on your device, and it is not recoverable if you uninstall the app, clear its storage, or lose the device.

3. Permissions the app uses

Permission or featureWhy Align uses it
NotificationsOptional. Asked only when you turn on daily reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent from any server. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings at any time.
Run after restartLets the app set your reminders again after your phone restarts. It collects nothing.
Photos and audioAlign does not request broad access to your photos, videos or files. When you add a photo to a vision board or import audio, Android's own file picker opens and shares only the item you choose with the app. It is processed on your device.

4. Purchases

Align offers optional paid features (Align Premium) as a weekly or 3-month subscription. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or bank details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of whether a purchase is active, and keeps that status on your device. Google processes purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play at any time.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information, because we do not have it. Information leaves your device only when you choose to export or share something yourself, for example by saving an audio file or image and sending it with another app. That other app's privacy policy then applies.

6. Third-party services

Align relies on two services provided by your device or Google: Google Play (for purchases) and the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. We do not control them. Please read their policies. Align does not include any third-party advertising or analytics code.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you control it:

  • delete individual journal entries, boards, scripts or subliminals inside the app;
  • to remove everything, open Android Settings, then Apps, then Align, then Storage, and choose Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

We keep no copy of your data, so we are unable to retrieve or delete it on your behalf. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

8. Security

Your information is kept in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's security model. Because Align makes no transfer of your personal data to us, there is no stored data on our side to be breached. No method of storage is completely secure, so we encourage you to protect your phone with a screen lock and to keep Android up to date.

9. Children

Align is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you are under the age of majority where you live, please use Align and make purchases only with the permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU and UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. As we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can exercise control over your data directly on your device as described in section 7. You may contact us with any privacy question or request at the address below.

11. International use

We do not transfer your personal data anywhere, because it stays on your device. Google Play purchases are handled by Google under its own policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add a feature that changes how information is handled. We will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app. If we ever add features that collect information, we will update this policy before releasing them. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
